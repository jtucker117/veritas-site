Common questions

The short answers.

What is Veritas Ventures?

A Texas holding company in Montgomery County. It owns and operates Veritas Builders today, with property management and a community nonprofit to follow.

What does Veritas Builders do?

Residential and commercial renovation, remodeling, new construction and site work as a general contractor across Greater Houston and Montgomery County.

What areas does Veritas serve?

Magnolia, Pinehurst, Tomball, Conroe, Montgomery, Willis, The Woodlands, Spring and the wider Greater Houston area.

Is Veritas Ventures locally owned?

Yes. It is locally rooted and relationship-driven, headquartered in Montgomery County, Texas.
